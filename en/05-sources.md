# Sources and further reading

A mix of source types, house-style: studies, industry reports, reference frameworks, and tools. Anything marked \[SOURCE PENDING\] is closed before publishing. No URL is invented: where the link isn’t confirmed, the source is described so you can find it.

### Frameworks and reference

- **Distributed Work’s Five Levels of Autonomy** · Matt Mullenweg (Automattic) · 2020 · the framework that pins which remote level you’re really at · ~15 min · blog ma.tt and TED talk.

- **State of Remote Work** · Buffer · annual · a snapshot of the field: what remote workers value and struggle with · ~20 min · Buffer’s public report.

- **State of Global Hiring 2026** · Deel · 2026 · real international hiring trends, contractor and EOR · ~25 min · Deel report.

- **The Remote Playbook** · Darren Murph et al. (GitLab) · 2021 · a full guide to remote transition and practice, from one of the largest all-remote companies · ~1 h.

- **The 10 models of remote and hybrid work** · GitLab Handbook · the full spectrum, from “no remote” to “strictly remote” · ~20 min · handbook.gitlab.com.

### Performance and metrics

- **Measure What Matters** · John Doerr · 2018 · the reference book on OKRs: how to tie objectives and KPIs to strategy · ~5 h.

- **High Output Management** · Andy Grove · 1983 · where measuring by output rather than activity comes from · ~5 h.

- **The Lean Startup** · Eric Ries · 2011 · where the term “vanity metric” comes from and why it kills dashboards · ~6 h.
