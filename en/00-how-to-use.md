# What this is and how to use it

This isn't a course or a slide deck. It's the bible: the reference document where everything we take for granted about remote work lives, in writing. If it isn't here, it isn't house doctrine — it's opinion.

It splits into two overviews that are the two sides of the same craft. The first looks from the seat of whoever builds and runs a remote company. The second, from the seat of whoever works in it without ever setting foot in an office. Read both even if only one applies to you: you don't understand your side until you understand the other.

### How to read it

- **Cover to cover the first time.** After that, dip in by section. The index up front links each one.

- **Every technical term is defined the first time it appears** and repeats in the lexicon at the end.

- **Data carries a source.** Anything marked \[SOURCE PENDING\] is a gap the faculty closes before publishing. Don't cite it until then.

- **Money is in US dollars (USD).** If a source gives another currency, it's converted and the original stays in parentheses.

It's a living document. It changes when the law, the platforms, or what we know changes. The good version is always the one on the campus.
